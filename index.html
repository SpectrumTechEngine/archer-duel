Open a challenge and your opponent can join it from this same page.

Press anywhere, drag back and release to loose an arrow. In open combat on one screen, each hunter draws on their own half. Step between your stakes with A and D, ← and →, or the arrows at your feet. The arrow that brings a bird down takes all its points: raven 1, pigeon 2, swallow 3, pheasant 5, goose 8, eagle 12. Pheasants, geese and eagles take two, three and four arrows. Five lone ravens open the hunt, then each level brings new birds and wilder flight. Strike the other hunter and he's knocked flat for three seconds.
